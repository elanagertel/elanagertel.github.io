In an Index course at Bezalel, I redesigned the Jerusalem Cinematheque's Israeli Film Archive. It's built for leisure, sorted by genres and holidays, but people come to archives to research history.

Searching for Mea Shearim footage from specific years, I couldn't reach them. Friends said they too came for specific periods, so years became the backbone.

The homepage is a grid: one row per decade. Years as a simple sort option made too large a mass. The film stays central, like looking through a time traveler's lens, with quiet white typography and hierarchy built from blinking and opacity.

My lecturer said it feels like being a living witness to past years. Next time, I'd add index filtering inside each year.
